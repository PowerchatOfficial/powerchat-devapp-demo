Send a chat message

202 means accepted, not displayed. The message still runs the streamer's moderation pipeline. This button reads chat/history back after ~1.5s to tell you whether it actually survived.

Report a view count

Re-post at least every 90s while live. A freshness sweep clears stale counts and your chip drops to 0 until the next report.

Fire a test alert

Display-only. App-fired alerts never credit goals, subathon time, leaderboards, or tip totals — they are for proving your overlay wiring.

Mint a tip checkout link

A fixed amount mints a single-use, one-hour intent: the URL carries only an opaque app_intent, so the viewer cannot edit the terms. Your ref comes back on the donation.completed webhook as appExternalRef.
